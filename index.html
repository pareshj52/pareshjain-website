Do you work with international clients?

Yes. I am based in India and open to suitable remote engagements with companies and teams internationally. We can agree on scope, communication cadence and working-time overlap up front.

Yes. I can support strategy, provide independent review, or coordinate delivery with your existing specialists. The goal is clearer ownership and stronger work, not unnecessary duplication.

Yes. The digital customer journey review is one example. For other engagements, I can assess the brief and recommend an appropriate fixed deliverable or a broader consulting scope.
