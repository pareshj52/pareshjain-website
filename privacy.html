Privacy

This site belongs to Paresh Jain, a freelance marketing consultant based in New Delhi, India. This page explains what information the site collects and what happens to it.

When you send an enquiry

The form collects your name, email, what you need, an optional budget range, an optional WhatsApp number and your message. It is delivered to my inbox through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co). I use it only to reply to you about your project. I don’t sell it or add you to a mailing list.

If you arrived from an ad or a tracked link, the form also sends the campaign details from that link (for example the source and campaign name) so I know which channels bring useful enquiries.

Analytics and advertising

The site uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages and buttons people use. When I run ads, it may also use Google Ads conversion tracking and the Meta Pixel to measure whether ads lead to enquiries. These services may set cookies.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked first, and these tools only set cookies after you accept (Google Consent Mode). Everywhere else they run by default. You can change your choice at any time, or block cookies in your browser; the site still works.

WhatsApp, email and calendar links

These open WhatsApp, your email app or Google Calendar’s booking page. Those services handle your information under their own privacy policies.

Keeping and deleting your information

I keep enquiry emails for as long as they’re useful for our conversation or project. To see or delete what I hold about you, email pareshj52@gmail.com.
