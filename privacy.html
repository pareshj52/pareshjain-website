Privacy

This site belongs to Paresh Jain, a freelance marketing consultant based in New Delhi, India. This page explains what information the site collects and what happens to it.

When you send a brief

The form collects your name, email, company, launch date and message. It is delivered to my inbox through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co). I use it only to reply to you about your project. I don’t sell it or add you to a mailing list.

If you arrived from an ad or a tracked link, the form also sends the campaign details from that link (for example the source and campaign name) so I know which channels bring useful enquiries.

Analytics and advertising

The site uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages and buttons people use. If I run ads, the site may also use the Meta Pixel to measure whether ads lead to enquiries. These services may set cookies. You can block them in your browser settings or with a content blocker, and the site will still work.

WhatsApp, email and calendar links

These open WhatsApp, your email app or Google Calendar’s booking page. Those services handle your information under their own privacy policies.

Keeping and deleting your information

I keep enquiry emails for as long as they’re useful for our conversation or project. To see or delete what I hold about you, email pareshj52@gmail.com.
