Is this right for you?

A good fit if

  • You have a launch, a festive push or a new product coming up
  • Your agency, creators and in-house team are each working from different ideas
  • You need a budget split and KPIs you can defend to leadership
  • You want a senior plan without hiring a full-time head of marketing

Probably not if

  • You only need posts made this week (an agency or freelancer is cheaper)
  • You want media buying handled with no plan behind it
