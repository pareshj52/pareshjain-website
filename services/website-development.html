Questions

Who builds the website?

I lead every project and review every deliverable. Specialist designers, editors and developers I brief and manage do the production.

What is an ‘AI website’?

A site built with modern AI-assisted, no-code tools such as Framer. It’s fast to build and easy to change, which suits landing pages and small sites.

Will I own the website?

Yes. The domain, hosting and store accounts are in your name, and you get all logins at handover.

What isn’t included?

Custom apps, complex integrations, large catalogue migrations and paid themes or plugins. I’ll quote separately or refer you to a specialist.

Can you also bring traffic?

Yes. Pair the site with a launch sprint, an ads audit or the monthly advisor package.
